Privacy notice

Your visit.
Your privacy.

A simple explanation of how this website handles your enquiry.

Illustrative photography · Nechekattu Farm

This static website prepares a visitor enquiry in your browser. It does not submit the form to a server, create an account, store enquiry details in browser storage, or include analytics or advertising trackers.

What you choose to share

The enquiry may include your name, email address, phone number, preferred date, group size, activities and additional notes. It is used to help the farm team respond to a visit request when you choose to send it.

Preparing and sending a message

Copying an enquiry places the message on your device’s clipboard. Downloading saves a text file on your device. If official contact details are configured, email and WhatsApp links open those services with your draft; sending is completed by you in the selected service.

External services

Google Maps, email, WhatsApp and any configured social links are external services with their own privacy practices. The site uses local copies of its images, fonts and animation libraries; visiting a page does not require loading those assets from third-party CDNs.

Contact and requests

For questions about any enquiry you have sent, contact the farm using the official details on the Visit Us page once available. Avoid sending sensitive personal data. The farm owner should confirm operational retention and contact arrangements before accepting live enquiries.
